# Simplification review

User feedback: text is too long, tasks are numerous and unclassified, reports should not link flare records, and full achievement is too difficult even for adults.

Authorized presentation cleanup: shorter mission/warning/report text, remove source links from play reports, retain scientific provenance in the separate Science Behind It page and data files, group assignments by purpose, preserve existing visual style.

Accepted and implemented on 15 November 2026: one two-step objective and three achievable stars; generator/fuel, optional discovery, cooling failures and reserve-check failures removed from Easy. Each stage transition adds 4 battery (capped at 90) and 2 food, while risk, shielding and objective progress persist. Retry restores the checkpoint after the single top-up, preventing supply farming. Medium/Hard retain their previous rules. Easy is the default selection.

The user's feedback supersedes the earlier goal that full achievement should be difficult. Success for the next revision is understandable choices and realistically achievable full stars, not higher punishment or additional systems.
