        Science Behind It

        Historical NASA observations, adapted into training decisions. This is not a forecast or a medical model.

        Source dataGame adaptation
        NASA DONKI flare and particle-event records, including identifiers, dates, instruments and links.Linked bundles are randomly placed after stage 1. Original dates are preserved. Warning timing is adapted, not a reconstruction of real advance warning.
        Several instrument detections can describe one episode.Shared flare links and observations within 48 hours are grouped into training clusters. These are not a catalogue of independent physical storms.
        Material shielding reduces radiation exposure.Game hit costs: 68 / 50 / 34 with passive layers; 1.5 in shelter. These are tuned risk points, NOT measured doses or mSv.
        Radiation monitoring informs operational restrictions.0.5 background risk per Earth day; outdoor work restricted at 40; evacuation at 100. These are game rules, not illness thresholds.
        The lunar solar cycle is about 29.5 Earth days.Medium/Hard use a simplified 28-day cycle; Easy is an eight-day training expedition crossing sunset.
        Space missions need food, energy and crew time.Task durations, robot repair rates, crop growth, system faults and resource budgets are authored teaching assumptions.

        Retrieved data

        The downloaded snapshot works offline. It is not live data. Missing event links do not prove that no real hazard occurred. The source records can contain later analyst revisions.
